# Prism Breaker share artwork

## Outputs

- `../public/assets/og-prism-breaker.jpg` — 1200 × 630, primary Open Graph/Twitter image.
- `../public/assets/og-prism-breaker-square.jpg` — 1200 × 1200, square Open Graph alternative.
- `../public/favicon.svg` — editable four-facet lime prism icon on charcoal.
- `../public/favicon.ico` — 16, 32 and 48 pixel PNG-backed frames.
- `../public/assets/favicon-32.png`, `../public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px), `../public/assets/prism-icon-192.png`, `../public/assets/prism-icon-512.png`.

The two poster compositions were made with the built-in imagegen tool, then mechanically exported to JPEG at the requested web dimensions. The favicon is repo-native SVG, rasterized from that vector; it was not generated by AI. Original generated PNGs are saved here as og-landscape-source.png and og-square-source.png. They are not published as runtime assets.

Regenerate exports from this game directory with `bun tools/build-share-assets.mjs`. The tool uses Sharp from the project dependencies after bun install. PRISM_IMAGE_MODULES can optionally select another dependency directory. New assets remain entirely hosted on Pagelove.

## Generation prompts

### Landscape

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Open Graph social share image for the actual browser game Prism Breaker. Create a polished LANDSCAPE game poster, exact composition ratio 1200:630 (about 1.9:1). Brand matches a colorful Three.js brick breaker game: deep blue-green charcoal #111d20, lime #b5ff6c, pink #f1a4df, icy cyan and warm gold. Large clean pale-green geometric sans-serif title on the LEFT, in two lines, exact text: "PRISM" then "BREAKER". Under the title exact small text "BREAK THROUGH." and lower-left understated exact text "A Pagelove Arcade Original". Keep all text comfortably inset with generous 60px-equivalent safe margins; exceptionally clear spelling and strong readable contrast. On the RIGHT, a luminous faceted octahedral lime prism floats above a slim paddle and a small glowing ball; a few rows of beveled rectangular rainbow bricks explode into elegant geometric shards and orbital light trails. The right-side object should be tangible 3D geometry, colorful physical materials, crisp facet edges, cinematic rim light and restrained bloom. A subtle grid and floating geometric fragments suggest the arcade arena. Composition is stylish, spare and balanced, vivid but not a noisy sci-fi collage. This is a finished promotional graphic, NOT a screenshot, NOT a fake browser interface. No people, no characters, no extra text, no watermark, no border. Render with a solid dark background.

### Square

Create a SQUARE 1:1 social share companion for this Prism Breaker promotional artwork. The provided landscape image is the visual and brand reference. Preserve its exact pale-lime geometric lettering style, deep blue-green charcoal background, lime octahedral prism, glass-like rainbow beveled bricks, paddle and luminous ball, and cinematic 3D lighting. Recompose for a square rather than cropping or squeezing the landscape: large two-line title at the TOP, exact text "PRISM" then "BREAKER"; a large glowing faceted lime prism in the MIDDLE with a few cyan, gold and pink bricks around it; the paddle and glowing ball in the LOWER middle. Under the title use exact text "BREAK THROUGH.". At the bottom use small exact text "A Pagelove Arcade Original". Maintain comfortable safe margins on all sides and a clear visual hierarchy. All text must be legible and spelled correctly. Keep the artwork polished and balanced with restrained light trails and a subtle floor grid. No new characters, no new copy, no watermark, no UI frame. Solid dark background. Finished square poster suitable for 1200 x 1200 social sharing.

The landscape was used as the square poster's visual reference.
