How scoring works.

Destroying a brick earns 100 × your combo multiplier. The combo counts bricks destroyed since the last paddle contact.

A 12-brick streak earns 2,000 points. Any ball touching the paddle resets the shared combo, including a Sticky catch. Losing all balls or starting a new board also resets it. Laser shots and explosion chains build that same combo; armored bricks score only when destroyed.

Clear bonus: 750 + 150 × lives remaining. Clear with three lives for 1,200 bonus points. The first two Citadel and Nexus formations award 500 each; the final formation uses the normal clear bonus. Earn an extra life every 12,000 points, up to nine lives.

Every collected power-up earns 150 points. Pickup points are added directly, without a combo multiplier. Multiball, Laser and Piercing can build brick combos. Wide, Slow and Shield help preserve lives. Sticky helps you aim, but each catch resets the combo. Each Extra life retained at a normal clear adds 150 to its bonus.

Speed bonus: up to 2,000 points for each board. Subtract 10 points for every whole active second: a 40-second clear earns 1,600; a 100-second clear earns 1,000; at 200 seconds the bonus reaches zero. Each Citadel and Nexus formation earns its own speed bonus. Leaderboards rank by score, then world reached, then shortest active play time. Pauses, cutscenes and waiting to launch after a life loss do not count toward time. Losing a life or saving and resuming does not reset the board timer.
