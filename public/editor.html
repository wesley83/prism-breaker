Keyboard shortcuts

V select · B paint · E erase · I sample · P play. Shift-click selects more bricks. Arrows nudge; Shift + arrows moves a grid cell. Delete removes a selection. Ctrl/Command + A selects all, D duplicates, Z undoes, Shift + Z redoes. Escape cancels a gesture or returns to Select.
